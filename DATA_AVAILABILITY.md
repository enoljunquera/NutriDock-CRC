
# Data availability

The processed datasets supporting the predictive analyses reported in NutriDock-CRC are publicly available in Zenodo (version 2):

https://zenodo.org/records/23728824

The released dataset includes pseudonymized subject-level dietary exposure matrices, food-level consumption features, molecular descriptors, combined feature matrices, diagnostic labels, and evaluation results for the 111 participants.

This GitHub repository provides the corresponding research scripts, molecular descriptors, and evaluation outputs. Some computational steps also depend on intermediate docking outputs or molecular structures that are not included in the public release.

The released subject-level datasets do not contain direct personal identifiers or raw clinical records. Data sharing was authorized for this research, and reuse must comply with the applicable dataset license and ethical requirements.

The redocking experiments assess structural pose recovery and should not be interpreted as direct measurements of enzyme kinetics, binding affinity, or metabolism.
